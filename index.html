Process · Shop app

01 · Research

User interviews and a look at competing shop apps, sorted on an affinity map. Three themes kept coming back.

02 · Sketch

The themes turn into a first pencil layout: personal picks up top, quick to draw and quick to throw away.

03 · Wireframe

Structure and hierarchy. What goes where, what scrolls sideways, what you can tap. The layout moves on from the sketch.

04 · Visual design

Colour, type and real content. Soft card colours per product, a serif for headings, a clean sans for everything else.

05 · Final screen

Every theme made it in: personal picks became “Just for you”, deals got their own row, saving for later became the Wishlist tab.
