Breath Natural

Breath Natural is a landing page concept for a houseplant shop, built around a moody, plant-filled hero image rather than the usual bright, minimal plant-shop look — letting the product photography do most of the talking.

A featured-plant card floats over the hero with quick add-to-cart access, and a customer review sits alongside it to build trust before the visitor even scrolls.
