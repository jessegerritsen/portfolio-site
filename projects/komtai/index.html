Komtai

Problem solved

A toaster oven that keeps the classic look people like, with convection cooking and a smarter way of managing its own heat inside.

Product design
01 The aim

Modern technology, in a classic shape

Komtai is a new oven toaster. The goal was to bring the latest oven technology into a toaster that keeps a classic style and stays friendly to use.

02 The 3D model

Turn it, open it, take it apart

The oven rebuilt as a 3D model you can handle. Drag to rotate, pull the slider to explode it into parts, or pick a mode to see the door open, the air move inside and the heat leave.

04 Convection

A fan moves the heat around the food

A convection oven has a fan that circulates air around the food, which gives very even heat. The extra circulation cooks food faster than a conventional oven without a fan, and the baking comes out smooth and consistent.

05 Keeping it cool

A blower and air valves manage the heat

A blower pulls heat out of the oven to control the temperature of the internal chamber. Air valves on the underside let air in and out around the inner chamber, which lowers the temperature of the oven body itself.

06 Controls

A touch display and two dials

A touch LCD shows the date and time, even in sleep mode, and lets you adjust temperature and time by touch. Two dials do the same job physically: TEMP sets the cooking temperature and TIME sets the cooking time.
