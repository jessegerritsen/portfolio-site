Snapp

Problem solved

With its user-friendly interface and quick delivery options, Snapp offers a better user experience and journey than its market competitors. Snapp provides a wider selection of goods at reasonable prices, keeping price, stock and variant selection visible without scrolling — the order-tracking and personalized "for you" screens carry that same visual language throughout.

Personal project
2 weeks
01 Low-fidelity exploration

Hand sketches first, iterated twice before any pixels

Every flow started on paper — Home, Profile, Ordered and Track, plus a five-step search-to-checkout sequence — sketched once, then iterated a second time once the gaps became obvious.

02 Low-fidelity wireframes

Home, Profile, Settings and Basket, then a six-screen search flow

The sketches were rebuilt as clickable low-fidelity wireframes, so the navigation and search flow could be tested before any visual design work began.

03 Site map

Mapping every screen before designing any of them

A full site map — from onboarding through search, categories, deals, cart, profile, orders and wishlist — kept every screen's navigation consistent once high-fidelity design started.

04 Final screens

Profile, orders, checkout and product detail — built out in full

The finished UI: account and interests, live order tracking, search, filtering, and the DualSense product page — with the cart sheet surfacing right where "Add to cart" leaves off.
